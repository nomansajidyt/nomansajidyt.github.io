Research Agenda

Questions I’m carrying
into graduate school

These themes currently organize my reading, experimentation, and conversations with potential advisors. They will evolve — that is part of the point of doctoral training.

01

Core Research Questions

What mechanisms drive the phenomena I care about? How can we design studies and models that reveal causal structure rather than surface correlations? I am interested in problems where careful measurement meets theoretical ambition.

02

Methods & Approaches

Combining analytical modeling, careful empirical design, and computational tools. I value transparency, reproducibility, and methods that scale with the complexity of the problem rather than forcing the problem to fit a convenient method.

03

Impact Horizon

Better understanding of these systems should inform both theory and real-world decisions. I want my work to be useful to the communities and fields that need it most — not only to other researchers.

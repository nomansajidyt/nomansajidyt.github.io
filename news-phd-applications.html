Opening PhD applications for Fall 2027

This cycle I am beginning formal outreach to potential advisors and programs with a target entry of Fall 2027. The focus remains on research environments that value careful question formation, methodological transparency, and collaboration across theory and application.

Much of the current work is practical: refining the research statement, organizing writing samples, and identifying labs whose recent work intersects with complex systems, computational methods, and rigorous empirical design. I am especially interested in groups that treat open science and reproducibility as part of everyday research practice.

If you are an advisor or current student working in related areas and would be open to a conversation about fit, methods, or the shape of doctoral training in your group, I would be glad to connect.

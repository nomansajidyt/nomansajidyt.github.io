Why a PhD

I want to spend years
on hard, important problems.

Doctoral training is the space where careful thinking, long-horizon questions, and intellectual community meet.

I am looking for an environment where deep technical rigor and intellectual curiosity are equally valued — and where the questions themselves are allowed to evolve.

My path has been shaped by a growing conviction that the most interesting problems live at the boundaries of disciplines. I am preparing to pursue a PhD so I can develop the tools, judgment, and collaborative capacity needed to contribute original work.

I am particularly drawn to research cultures that value both theoretical clarity and practical impact. Berkeley’s tradition of open, ambitious, and cross-cutting inquiry is a strong fit for the kind of researcher I aim to become.

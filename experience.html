Looking ahead

What I’m seeking in a PhD

An advisor and lab culture that values both intellectual risk-taking and careful craft — where students are treated as junior colleagues.

I am looking for a program and research group where I can develop deep expertise while remaining connected to adjacent fields. Ideal environments encourage students to propose ideas, receive rigorous feedback, and grow into independent researchers.

Practical priorities include strong methodological training, opportunities for collaboration across groups, and a culture of open science and reproducibility. Location and funding structure matter, but intellectual fit comes first.

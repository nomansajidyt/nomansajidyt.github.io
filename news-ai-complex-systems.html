Notes on AI and complex systems

A recurring theme in my reading is the difference between models that predict well and models that help us understand mechanism. Modern AI systems are powerful at the first task; the second remains harder and, for many scientific questions, more important.

Complex systems—whether social, biological, or technological—often exhibit structure that is not obvious from surface statistics. I am interested in how computational tools, including carefully designed machine learning approaches, can be used to probe that structure: to propose interventions, test alternative mechanisms, and make assumptions explicit rather than burying them in black boxes.

This is not an argument against predictive methods. It is an argument for pairing them with clear research design, transparent evaluation, and theory that can be revised when evidence accumulates. Those habits are what I hope to develop further in doctoral training.
